Anointed Acres Stables

"Empowering Families Through Equine Connection"

At Anointed Acres Stables, the journey is rooted in a mission of empowering families and at-risk youth through profound equine connection. Their specialized character development programs combine horsemanship with therapeutic farmwork, teaching children and families to navigate life's challenges with newfound confidence and purpose.

Civil Rights & Agriculture · Est. 1995

National Black Farmers Association

"Fighting for the Rights of Black Farmers Nationwide"

Founded in 1995 by 4th-generation farmer and civil rights activist John W. Boyd, Jr., the NBFA is headquartered right here in Baskerville, VA — just minutes from the RTTLC estate. The organization advocates for land retention, equitable access to USDA programs, agricultural training, and rural economic development for tens of thousands of Black farmers across America. They've led marches on the White House, testified before Congress, and secured landmark settlements against USDA discrimination.

📍 Baskerville, VA (Brunswick County) 🌾 Tens of Thousands of Members Nationwide 🌐 blackfarmers.org

Advocacy & Services

⚖️ Civil Rights Advocacy 🏡 Land Retention 📋 USDA Program Access 🌱 Agricultural Training 🏗️ Rural Economic Development 💰 Farm Loan Assistance 📢 Federal Policy Advocacy

Full Circle Bloom

"Growing Clean, Growing Local, Growing Community"

Full Circle Bloom is a Black-woman and veteran-owned aeroponic farm in Kenbridge, VA (Lunenburg County — right next door to Brunswick). Founded by Ashley Richardson, a former Senior Project Manager who traded the D.C. corporate world for the soil. Their cutting-edge aeroponic systems grow pesticide-free produce year-round. They've donated over 25,000 pounds of fresh produce to local schools, food banks, and community organizations — proving that tech and soul can grow together.

📍 Kenbridge, VA (Lunenburg County) 🏆 Microsoft Southern VA Fund Recipient 🎖️ Tractor Supply Farmer Veteran Coalition 🌐 fullcirclebloom.com

VSU Small Farm Outreach Program

"Empowering Small, Minority, and Veteran Farmers"

The Small Farm Outreach Program (SFOP) at Virginia State University is a critical resource for small-scale, socially disadvantaged, and minority farmers across the Commonwealth. Through Virginia Cooperative Extension, they provide hands-on technical assistance, educational workshops on whole farm planning, mentorship connecting new farmers with experienced producers, and direct help navigating USDA FSA loans and grants like REAP and VAPG. This is the bridge between dream and deed.

📍 Petersburg, VA (VSU Randolph Farm) 📞 (804) 524-3292 📧 smallfarm@vsu.edu 🌐 ext.vsu.edu
