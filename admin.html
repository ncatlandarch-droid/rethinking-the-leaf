Financial Management Recommendations

Options for tracking money in & out

🏆 Recommended: Wave (Free)

Best fit for RTTL right now. Wave is completely free accounting software for small businesses — no credit card needed. It handles invoicing, receipt scanning, financial reports, and connects to your PayPal & bank accounts.

  • Cost: $0/month (free forever for accounting & invoicing)
  • PayPal integration: Auto-imports PayPal donations
  • Reports: Profit & loss, balance sheet, sales tax
  • Receipt scanning: Phone app scans receipts → auto-categorizes
  • Best for: Pre-revenue / early-stage businesses exactly like RTTL
  • Website: waveapps.com

💼 Alternative: QuickBooks Simple Start ($20/mo)

Industry standard, but costs money. Better when you have regular revenue flowing and need payroll, inventory tracking, or tax prep exports for an accountant.

  • Cost: $20/month (Simple Start plan)
  • Best for: When RTTL starts generating $1K+/month
  • Payroll add-on: $45/month extra when you hire staff

📱 Alternative: This Dashboard (Custom — Free)

What you're looking at right now! A built-in lightweight tracker. Good for visibility across the team, but not a replacement for proper accounting software. Use this for team coordination and Wave/QB for actual bookkeeping.

  • Cost: $0 — built into your website
  • Limitations: Manual data entry (edit data.json), no bank sync
  • Best for: Team visibility, task tracking, project planning
  • Future: Can evolve into a full app with database if needed

💡 Recommended Setup

Use both together:

  • Wave → actual bookkeeping, PayPal sync, financial reports, taxes
  • This Dashboard → team task board, capital project tracking, team directory, quick financial overview

As RTTL grows, upgrade Wave → QuickBooks and this dashboard → a custom farm management app.
