Part 1: The Vision — Integrated Agrotherapy Campus

The Big Idea

The elderly care facility is not a separate project — it is the keystone that makes the entire 66-acre estate self-sustaining. The lavender farm becomes therapeutic infrastructure for elderly residents. The training programs produce workers for both the farm AND the facility. The farm feeds the facility. The facility funds the farm.

👥 Intergenerational Programming — Communal Across Generations

Core Philosophy

Young people and elderly residents work TOGETHER — not separately. Trainees help manage programs that serve residents. Residents mentor young people with life experience and wisdom. This communal model is how farming communities have always functioned — elders teach, youth do the heavy lifting, and everyone eats together.

🏛️ 501(c)(3) Nonprofit Strategy — Hybrid Structure

Critical Decision

RTTLC should operate a hybrid model — a for-profit corporation for revenue operations AND a 501(c)(3) nonprofit foundation for elder care, community service, and grant eligibility. This is standard practice in agrotourism and healthcare and opens the most funding doors.

Filing Steps — 501(c)(3) Application

  • Step 1: Form a Virginia nonprofit corporation through the SCC (~$100, 1–2 weeks)
  • Step 2: Draft Articles of Incorporation with required IRS charitable purpose language
  • Step 3: Create Bylaws, appoint Board of Directors (minimum 3 members)
  • Step 4: Obtain EIN from IRS (free, same-day online)
  • Step 5: File IRS Form 1023 or 1023-EZ for tax-exempt status (~$275–$600 fee)
  • Step 6: Wait for IRS determination letter (3–6 months; 1023-EZ can be faster)
  • Step 7: Register with Virginia Department of Agriculture (charitable solicitation registration)

Part 4: Chronic Guru — Business Model Analysis

What Is Chronic Guru?

Chronic Guru is a cannabis/hemp dispensary brand — not a health/wellness company. Founded in 2016 by Patrick O'Brien (a veteran and third-generation cannabis grower) and co-founded with Erdem Sezer, headquartered in Apopka, Florida.

They sell THCa flower, edibles, concentrates, pre-rolls, THC-infused drinks, and vape products — marketing themselves as "farm-direct," organic, and family-owned.

⚠️ Critical Legal Warning

Federal legislation (H.R. 5371) signed November 2025 shifts hemp's legal definition to a "total THC" standard (counting both Delta-9 THC AND THCa). Effective November 12, 2026 — after this date, high-THCa products will be classified as marijuana. Many states have already enacted bans ahead of the federal deadline.

Recommendation

Lamont is right to be cautious. The model has been widely copied — it is NOT proprietary. The legal window closes in ~5 months (Nov 2026). Pivoting to state-licensed marijuana operations requires much heavier regulation, taxation, and capital. We recommend focusing energy on the elderly care idea instead — it has real long-term viability and strong grant support.
