Community Served

ReThinking The Leaf Corporation is building an integrated agrotherapy campus in Brunswick County, Virginia — one of the most underserved communities in the state. Our vision connects lavender farming, hemp health products, intergenerational community programs, and healing into one self-sustaining system on historically significant Black-owned land.

Your donation directly funds: community wellness center programs, farm-to-table nutrition, intergenerational training, cedar sauna wellness, community meals for neighborhood seniors, and food distribution to food-insecure families.
